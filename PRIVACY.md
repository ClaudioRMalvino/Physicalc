# Privacy Policy — Physicalc

_Last updated: 11 June 2026_

Physicalc is a physics reference and study app developed by Claudio Ricardo Malvino.

## The short version

**Physicalc collects no data. None.**

## Details

- The app requests **no permissions** and makes **no network connections** of any kind.
- All content (equations, definitions, solvers, the unit converter, flashcards,
  and the solar-system display) is bundled with the app and works entirely offline.
- Your only stored datum is your **theme preference** (dark/light), saved locally
  on your device. It never leaves your device.
- No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no accounts, no third-party SDKs
  that collect data.

## Open source

Physicalc is open source under the MIT License. You can verify every claim above
by reading the code: https://github.com/ClaudioRMalvino/Physicalc

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the GitHub repository above.
