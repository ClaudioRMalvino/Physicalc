## Full description (max 4000 chars)
Physicalc is a physics reference and study companion for first-year university
physics, following the openly licensed OpenStax University Physics Volume 1
textbook (chapters 3–14).

EVERYTHING, OFFLINE
No account, no ads, no permissions, no network. The whole app works in a
lecture hall basement, on a plane, or anywhere else physics happens.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• 149 equations across 12 chapters — kinematics, Newton's laws, work and
  energy, momentum and collisions, rotation, angular momentum, equilibrium
  and elasticity, gravitation, and fluid dynamics — each with every variable
  defined and its units stated, typeset like a textbook.

• 58 interactive solvers — pick the variable you want, enter what you know,
  and get the answer, with honest error handling (no real roots, division by
  zero, negative time…).

• 165 plain-language definitions of the key terms of each chapter.

• Flashcards with self-graded recall: study equations or definitions chapter
  by chapter, flip the card, judge yourself, get a score.

• A live unit converter for length, time, mass, force, energy, pressure and
  speed that converts as you type.

• A home screen with a real model of the solar system: each planet is drawn
  at its true position for today's date, computed from JPL orbital elements
  by solving Kepler's equation. Plus a physics quote of the day.

• Dark theme and a calm paper-cream light theme.

OPEN SOURCE
Physicalc is MIT-licensed. The code — including the full test suite that
verifies every calculation — is public:
https://github.com/ClaudioRMalvino/Physicalc

Physics content is referenced from OpenStax University Physics Volume 1,
an openly licensed textbook.
